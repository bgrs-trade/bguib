About The Bureau

Driving Sustainable Urbanization & Modern Infrastructure Development

Established under the proclamation of the Benishangul-Gumuz Regional State Council, the Urban and Infrastructure Bureau leads regional urban planning, land administration modernization, construction industry regulation, and municipal infrastructure expansion to elevate the quality of life for all citizens.

Our Vision (ራዕይ)

To see competitive, green, resilient, and inclusive cities in Benishangul-Gumuz supported by world-class urban infrastructure and transparent digital land governance by 2030.

Our Mission (ተልዕኮ)

Accelerate equitable urban growth by preparing modern structural plans, enforcing construction quality standards, expanding integrated road and housing networks, and delivering citizen-centric e-services.
